Budget Jars Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Budget Jars is a personal budgeting app. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

What the app stores

The jars you create (names, monthly limits, icons and colors) and the spends you log (amounts, notes and dates) are saved on your device only. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use the internet to work, and it does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, photos or files.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account, under Google's own privacy terms. You can turn this off in your device's backup settings.

Deleting your data

Delete jars and spends inside the app at any time, or clear everything by uninstalling the app or using Settings → Apps → Budget Jars → Storage → Clear storage.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
